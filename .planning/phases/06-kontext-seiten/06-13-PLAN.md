Close the formal verification gap of Phase 6 (06-VERIFICATION.md `gaps:`, review finding CR-01) and the bundled warning WR-04, both in `app/src/lib/ruecklagen.ts`:

- CR-01 (BLOCKER): The footnote under the Rücklagen table on `/entwicklung` explains the "Rückgang im Jahr" without the Verrechnung der Bilanzierungshilfe. The implemented formula (same as Vorbericht S. 23 and `pipeline/ostbevern/texte.py::_allgemeine_ruecklage_abbau`) is `max(0, −Jahresergebnis − Ausgleichsrücklage) − Verrechnung`. A reader applying the footnote gets 0,56 % for 2026 while table, chart and PDF S. 23 show 1,77 %. Fix: derive the footnote clause from the formula constants in `ruecklagen.ts`, name the Verrechnung term (with the printed posten name from the data) whenever the data contains a non-zero Verrechnung, and prove by test that the footnote reproduces every shown value.
- WR-04: `baueRuecklagen()` presents a partial sum as "Summe" when one Rücklage is missing (`?? 0`). Fix: `summe` is `null` unless both halves exist.

Purpose: Core Value ("Jede Zahl ... korrekt aus dem Haushalts-PDF abgeleitet") and ENTW-03 / D-14 ("Wie lange reicht das Polster?") — every shown percentage must be reproducible from the text next to it, and no total may silently treat a missing value as 0.
Output: `rueckgangFormelText()` with tests, rewired footnote in `EntwicklungPage.vue`, `baueRuecklagen()` without partial sums.
