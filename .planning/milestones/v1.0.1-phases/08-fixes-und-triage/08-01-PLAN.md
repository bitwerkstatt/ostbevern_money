Make every year number in the curated explanation texts a data placeholder and let the text gate reject hand-typed years (TXT-03, 05/IN-10, D-01 to D-05).

Output: stricter `pruefe_text`, new `pruefe_titel`, new `jahr.`-keys and the `jahr.fest_JJJJ` resolver in `texte.py`; seven converted sections in `erklaerungen.md`; regenerated `texte.json`; `istJahrneutral` rule in the app; pytest and vitest cases.
</objective>
