Planning-time facts (verified 2026-10-01 against raw_data/haushalt-2026.pdf with pdfplumber 0.11.10 and the committed daten/):
- Seitenbereich `querschnitte` covers S. 291-300. Every PB has an Ergebnisplan block followed by a Finanzplan block. Block titles (Textzeile.text) look like `01 Innere Verwaltung - Haushaltsquerschnitt Ergebnisplan`. Quirks: PB names differ between blocks and from hierarchie.csv (`02 Sicherheit und Ordnung` vs `02 Innere Sicherheit`, typo `16 Allgemeinde Finanzwirtschaft`); `12 Verkehrsflächen und -anlagen- Haushaltsquerschnitt Ergebnisplan` has no space before the dash; the PB 09 Finanzplan title on S. 296 is only `09 Räumliche Planung und Entwicklung, Geoinformationen` (no "Haushaltsquerschnitt Finanzplan"). Therefore: take only the two-digit PB code from the title (pattern `^(?P<pb>\d{2}) \D` on Textzeile.text) and derive the plan type from the header column count.
- Header: the first header line starts with the word `Produktgruppen` followed by exactly one word per column — 7 for the Ergebnisplan (`Ordentliche Ordentliche Ordentliches Finanzergebnis Ergebnisderlfd. Außerordentliches Ergebnisdes`), 11 for the Finanzplan (`Einzahlun- Auszah- Saldoaus Einzahlun- Auszah- Saldoaus Finanzmittel- Einzahlun- Auszah- Saldoaus Verpflich-`). Further header lines (2 for Ergebnisplan, 4-5 for Finanzplan, e.g. `in C Erträge ...`, `tätigkeit tätigkeit`) carry no amounts.
- Data rows: the first word is the PG code glued to its name, e.g. `0101PolitischeGremien`; every column is printed (zeros as `0`); names wrap onto a next line without amounts (`Mann`, `nagement`, `nung`, `zogeneOrdnungsmaßnahmen`). `GESAMTSUMME` closes each block.
- Amounts are right-aligned: an amount's x1 lies 5-17 pt right of the x1 of its column's word on the first header line; the smallest column pitch is about 51 pt (Finanzplan) and 70 pt (Ergebnisplan), so nearest-x1 assignment with tolerance = half the smallest anchor pitch is unambiguous.
- PB 01 Finanzplan continues: S. 291 ends with `Fortsetzungfolgt...` (no GESAMTSUMME yet); S. 292 starts directly with the bare `Produktgruppen` header, then PG 0112 and GESAMTSUMME. Footer: the Anlagen page number (e.g. `1`, `3`, `6`, `10`) is a line of one integer word near the bottom; note `1` passes `ist_betrag`, so it must be skipped before amount detection.
- The pages are landscape and the PDF page number is printed at the far LEFT margin (x0 about 13, x1 about 23), left of the row-label column (x0 about 43-44). On most pages it forms its own line, but on S. 293 it shares the `top` of the PB 03 Finanzplan row of PG 0302 and becomes that line's FIRST word (`293 0302Schülerbeförderung 10.000 ...`). Without removing it, PG 0302 is silently lost (the only mismatch found in a planning-time scan: 1141 instead of 1152 values). Rule: before classifying any line, drop a word whose text equals `str(pdf_seite)` and whose x1 lies left of the leftmost x0 of the page's title, header and data-row words; then a line that became empty or consists of one integer word is a footer.

- Kennzahl mapping verified against ergebnisplan.csv: "Ergebnis des Teilhaushaltes" equals TP Z. 26 (Jahresergebnis), NOT Z. 29 — PG 0102 Ansatz Haushaltsjahr: Z. 26 = -178.200 = Querschnitt, Z. 29 = -174.000.
